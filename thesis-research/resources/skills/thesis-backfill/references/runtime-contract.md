# Thesis Runtime Contract 1.0

This is the compact model-facing contract compiled into stage prompts. The
shared Thesis core contract and deterministic validators remain authoritative.

## Admission

- Public content needs a verified investable object and ticker, the tracked
  author's own judgment (`what`), a non-technical reason (`why`) and an explicit
  investment landing.
- A Timeline update independently needs its own ticker set, direction, what,
  why and material reason/evidence/condition/correction increment.
- Technical levels, trades, holdings, performance, objective results, news and
  generic praise remain private unless an independent company-specific what and
  why survives after removing them.
- No minimum length, target, stop, follower count or multi-post history applies.

## Identity And History

- One author plus one resolved company has one active Thesis. Business lines,
  risks, catalysts and trade episodes stay in that history.
- Preserve every source, date, correction, reversal and distinct event. Another
  author's view is context or Signal, never the tracked author's Timeline.
- Every source has exactly one disposition. A complete triage candidate becomes
  public, pending, or an evidence-bound hard exclusion.

## Evidence

- Preserve subject, owner, predicate, polarity, certainty, degree, condition,
  units, transaction stage and time scope.
- Every public claim and ticker direction maps to exact original evidence. Never
  add later evidence to an earlier event or reasons from another expression.
- If evidence is insufficient, hold or omit; never guess to fill a schema.

## Expression-Local Data

- Every visible expression has at least one verified ticker and exactly one
  source-backed `bullish`, `bearish` or `none` direction per displayed ticker.
- Tickers, directions and media belong to that exact expression. They are never
  inherited from the current Thesis.
- Inventory every attachment on expression and quote/reply context sources.
  Resolve each as include, omit or retrieval gap with an image-specific reason.

## Language

- Sentence one states the specific investment direction and decisive mechanism.
  Ticker metadata never supplies the visible stance.
- Use Bullish/Bearish/Neutral only when the exact source expression uses the same
  word and the sentence also contains its mechanism.
- Preserve source-date certainty and conditions. Do not expose review/process or
  portfolio-operation language. Do not generate titles.
- Each current composed passage and dated event is at most 500 characters before
  named original links.
- Review the actual temporal Feed order. Adjacent or concentrated surface
  families fail even across different Thesis records. Change the source-owned
  judgment structure, not cosmetic synonyms.

## Temporal Feed

- Every eligible historical expression appears once in the chronological Feed.
- Opening a dated item shows that exact event at the top and only strictly
  earlier events below. Never duplicate the selected event or leak future facts.

## Stage Boundary

1. Triage identifies candidates but writes no prose.
2. Source review produces `reviewed-thesis-facts/1.0` with grouping, evidence,
   event dispositions, tickers, directions and media inventory.
3. Language generation consumes only approved facts.
4. Final review checks the exact rendered projection and hash.

Publication, notification and schedules remain separate authorized operations.
