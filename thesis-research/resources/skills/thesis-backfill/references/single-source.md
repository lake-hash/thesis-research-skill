# One Displayed Source Per Expression

This user-confirmed rule supersedes earlier multi-source footer instructions for
the Thesis Playbook. Each current thesis and Timeline update displays exactly one
named original hyperlink. Its header date and sorting date use that same source.

The current card summarizes the selected dated expression, not a synthesis of
separate posts from different dates. Keep the company grouping and earlier events
in Timeline. Do not merely hide supporting links while retaining claims from
those other posts. Exact source evidence and contextual attribution remain private.

Read contemporaneous reply/quote context to interpret the selected expression,
without assigning other speakers' claims to its author. A complete interview or
one revision of a report can be one source; its turns/pages do not require extra
visible links. Preserve their internal locators. Never import later facts.

Select a source that supports the complete displayed summary, then bind source,
date and prose together. Exclude that expression from visible Timeline. Retain
500-character prose and the 40-word Timeline preview. Check rendered card and
historical-detail footers for exactly one link, with the same URL as their header.
